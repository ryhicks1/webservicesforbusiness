03 — How it works

Four steps. No mystery.

Same shape whether it's a website, an AI assessment, or both together.

STEP 01

Enquire

Call or send the form. Tell me what you do and what's annoying you. No charge and no pitch deck.

STEP 02

Scope

We agree what's actually needed and what it costs, in writing, before any work starts.

STEP 03

Build or advise

I build the site, or run the assessment and the sessions. You see it as it goes, not at the end.

STEP 04

Handover

You own the domain, the site and the accounts. I show you how to run it. Help afterwards if you want it.

04 — AI, without the theatre

Practical AI for small Australian businesses.

No transformation slogans. I work with what you've already got — your quotes, your inbox, your scheduling, your content — and we make one or two jobs measurably faster.

01

AI readiness assessment

Where you are today: the tools you're paying for, where your data lives, what the risks are, and the quick wins sitting in front of you. You get written findings and a priority list you can act on without me.

02

Team AI education

Hands-on sessions for owners and staff. ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor basics, what's safe to put in and what isn't, and prompts written around the jobs your people actually do — using your own work as the examples.

03

Workflow design

Pick one painful process — quoting, follow-ups, scheduling, content — and we map it properly, then build a simple AI-assisted version of it. One process done well beats ten half-automated.

04

Tool choice & setup

What to buy, what to cancel, and how to configure it. Set up under your own accounts so nothing is hostage to me, and no vendor lock-in theatre.

05

Ongoing advisory Optional

A light monthly check-in for teams that want a steady hand as things change. Month to month — cancel whenever it stops being useful.

Who it's for

  • Owners and small teams, roughly 1–30 people
  • Trades, clinics, retail, professional services
  • People who've tried ChatGPT once and want to know what's next
  • Teams worried about doing it unsafely with client data

What you get

  • Written findings and a ranked list of what to do first
  • Sessions run on your real work, not generic demos
  • Working setups in your own accounts
  • Plain notes your team can follow after I've gone

What it's not

  • Not enterprise change management
  • Not a chatbot bolted onto your website
  • Not a plan to replace your staff
  • Not a subscription you forget you're paying for
